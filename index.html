What DecodeFood does

Everything you need to read a label like a food scientist.

Scan any label

Advanced OCR reads ingredient lists directly from packaging — curved bottles, glossy bags, and everything in between.

Personalized diet profiles

Choose from 8 diet modes. Add allergens, intolerances and custom flags. Your profile never leaves your device.

Science-based explanations

Every flagged ingredient gets a plain-language breakdown: what it is, why it's there, and what it does to your body.

Privacy first

No account required. Your dietary profile is stored locally and never transmitted to any server. Anonymous usage analytics help us improve the app — no personal data, ever.

NOVA processing scale

Identifies ultra-processed foods using the scientific NOVA framework, with specific reasons — not arbitrary scores.

Barcode + OCR fallback

Scans the barcode first for instant lookup, then falls back to direct OCR analysis when there's no database match. You can also type a UPC manually.

The science behind it

Every ingredient explained by the data, not the marketing.

When DecodeFood flags an ingredient, it tells you exactly why — in plain English, backed by regulatory and scientific sources.

  1. What it is — the actual substance and how it's made
  2. Why it's in there — preservative, thickener, flavoring, filler
  3. What it does — the honest clinical picture for your body
  4. Your call — we give you the facts, you make the decision
Our philosophy

Data, Not Fear.

Most food apps give you a colored score and tell you what to think. We don't. DecodeFood is an objective translation tool — it converts chemical names and processing jargon into plain English so you can make your own informed decision. We don't label foods "good" or "bad." We don't push you toward buying alternatives. We give you the facts, and we trust you with them.

"The expert on your own body is you.
We're just here to decode the label."
